Multi-camera guide

How to record with several iPhones at once from one Mac

By Mohsin Sapra · Published October 8, 2026 · About 5 min read

One angle gets old. A second or third camera gives you cutaways, a wide shot, a close-up and a safety take. Most people already own more than one iPhone in the house, or can borrow one.

The hard part is managing them. Walking to each phone to start recording and check focus is slow. A single Mac window fixes that.

Quick answer

Install CamBridge Camera on each iPhone and CamBridge Studio on the Mac. Studio shows all phones in a grid. Click a tile for a program view of that camera. Each phone has its own controls and its own record button. Each recording goes to that phone's subfolder in your Footage folder.

When a second phone is worth it

Two angles let you cut on a sentence break and hide mistakes. A wide shot plus a close-up gives a talk a more finished feel. A third phone on the desk can capture your hands or a product.

Plan the angles first. Put the main phone straight on, the second at 30 to 45 degrees, and keep the lenses at different focal lengths so cuts look intentional. Studio's grid lets you check all of them at once before you press record.

Step by step with CamBridge

You need two or more iPhones on iOS 17+ with CamBridge Camera, a Mac on macOS 14+ with Studio, and a shared Wi‑Fi network. A USB cable per phone is optional.

  1. Install on every phone

    Install CamBridge Camera on each device and open it.

  2. Put everything on the same network

    Connect all phones and the Mac to the same Wi‑Fi. For one or two phones you can also use a cable. Studio detects it automatically.

  3. Open Studio

    Each phone appears as a tile in the multi-phone grid. Give them clear positions: wide, medium, close.

  4. Set up each camera

    Click a tile to open the program view for that phone. Focus, set exposure, choose lens and frame rate. Controls are per phone. Repeat for each.

  5. Match the settings

    Use the same frame rate and resolution on every phone to make editing easier. Lock exposure on each so the shots do not shift.

  6. Record

    Start and stop each phone from Studio. Each one records its own real 4K file on the device.

  7. Collect the files

    Files copy to your chosen Footage folder, in per-phone subfolders. Import them all into your editor. Use sync-by-audio or a clap to align them.

  8. Optional: Director mode

    You can use a second iPhone as a monitor and remote instead of the Mac. That helps when you are away from the desk.

Without CamBridge

  • Record each phone by hand. Free. You must start each phone, and you will not see all shots at once.
  • Multi-cam apps with Wi‑Fi sync. Several third-party camera apps exist. Compare features before choosing. Check what leaves the phone and where.
  • A video-call tool. Everyone joins a call and you record it. Quality is webcam-grade, and the call app decides the resolution.
  • Camera system. Mirrorless cameras and a switcher are better in many ways, and cost far more.

Tips and troubleshooting

  • Name each phone clearly so subfolders make sense.
  • Check storage on every phone before you start. 4K fills space quickly.
  • Keep phones plugged into power for long sessions.
  • Use a cable for the phones you move least, and Wi‑Fi for the rest. A busy network shows up as a choppy preview.
  • Record the audio once, on the best microphone. See the Mac mic guide.

Limits to know about

CamBridge is not a live switcher or a streaming tool. It does not produce a mixed program output. The grid is for monitoring and control. Recording is per phone, and you edit afterwards. How many phones one Mac can handle depends on the Mac and the network. I have not published a tested maximum.

FAQ

How many iPhones can I connect?

Studio supports several in a grid. The practical number depends on your Mac and network.

Are the clips synced to each other?

Not automatically. Sync them in your editor with audio or a clap.

Can I record all phones with one button?

Studio gives per-phone controls and recording. Check the current build for any group action.

Where do the files go?

To your Footage folder, in per-phone subfolders.

Can I use different iPhone models?

Yes, as long as each runs iOS 17 or later. Features vary by model.

What is Director mode?

A second iPhone used as a monitor and remote.
