Continuity Camera vs CamBridge: which to use (and why iPhone Mirroring can't open the Camera)

By Mohsin Sapra · Published October 8, 2026 · About 6 min read

People often search for a way to use the iPhone camera with a Mac and land on three Apple features: Continuity Camera, iPhone Mirroring and QuickTime. They solve different problems.

This guide explains what each does, where CamBridge fits, and when you should not use CamBridge at all.

Quick answer

Use Continuity Camera when a Mac app needs a webcam: Zoom, FaceTime, a streaming tool. Use CamBridge when you want to record on the iPhone and monitor and control it from the Mac. iPhone Mirroring shows the phone screen but, per Apple, access to the iPhone camera and mic is not available in it.

The short version of each tool

Continuity Camera makes your iPhone a camera input for Mac apps. It is a good, simple, built-in feature for video calls. iPhone Mirroring is for using the phone's apps from the Mac. CamBridge is for filming with the iPhone and managing the shoot from the Mac. The three overlap less than the names suggest.

Step by step with CamBridge

Here is the comparison, then a short decision path.

  1. Ask: is this for a call or stream?

    If yes, use Continuity Camera (or Camo). CamBridge is not a webcam or virtual camera. It will not appear in Zoom, FaceTime or OBS.

  2. Ask: do I need the camera file itself?

    If you want the iPhone's real 4K recording, shot on the phone, choose CamBridge. Continuity Camera gives apps a webcam stream.

  3. Ask: do I need remote control?

    CamBridge adds click to focus, exposure, zoom, lenses, torch, frame rate and record from the Mac. Continuity Camera hands the phone to the Mac app, with Apple's video effects such as Center Stage and Desk View.

  4. Ask: is this vertical?

    CamBridge lets you choose portrait or landscape capture. Continuity Camera is mounted in landscape or portrait per Apple, but apps usually treat it as a wide webcam feed.

  5. Ask: how many cameras?

    CamBridge shows several iPhones in one Studio grid. Continuity Camera uses one iPhone as the Mac's camera input.

  6. Use both if needed

    They can live on the same Mac. Use Continuity Camera for the call, CamBridge for the shoot.

Without CamBridge

What Apple says about Continuity Camera

Apple lists these requirements: iOS 16 or later on an iPhone XR or later, macOS Ventura 13 or later, and both devices signed in to the same Apple Account with two-factor authentication. It works wired or wirelessly. Video features include Desk View, Center Stage and Studio Light (Apple support: Continuity Camera).

Why iPhone Mirroring can't open the Camera

iPhone Mirroring needs macOS Sequoia 15 or later and iOS 18 or later. Apple's support page says: "Access to the iPhone camera and mic is not available in iPhone Mirroring, including for Face ID or phone calls." It is also currently unavailable in the European Union (Apple support: iPhone Mirroring). So you cannot mirror the phone and use its Camera app from the Mac. That is a design choice by Apple, not a setting you can change.

QuickTime and other options

  • QuickTime Player. It can record a connected iPhone. It has no remote control of the phone camera.
  • Camo. A webcam and virtual camera tool for calls and streams. See CamBridge vs Camo.
  • Blackmagic Camera. A pro iPhone camera app. See CamBridge vs Blackmagic Camera.

Tips and troubleshooting

  • Do not expect a webcam preview in Zoom from CamBridge. Use the right tool for each job.
  • If you want a vertical monitor, see the vertical video guide.
  • If Continuity Camera does not show up, check Apple's list: same Apple Account, Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi on, and no AirPlay or Sidecar use for wireless.

Limits to know about

CamBridge is a beta with no Android or Windows version, and no webcam mode. Continuity Camera needs recent Apple software and an Apple Account on both devices. CamBridge needs no account.

FAQ

Is CamBridge a replacement for Continuity Camera?

No. They do different jobs. Continuity Camera is for calls and apps that need a webcam. CamBridge is for recording and remote control.

Can I open the iPhone Camera app through iPhone Mirroring?

Apple says access to the iPhone camera and mic is not available in iPhone Mirroring.

Does Continuity Camera record 4K?

Check Apple's page and your Mac app. Apple frames it as a webcam. CamBridge records 4K on the phone.

Can I use both?

Yes.

Does CamBridge need an Apple Account sign-in?

CamBridge has no accounts. TestFlight itself uses your Apple Account.

Does CamBridge work for Zoom?

No. Use Continuity Camera or Camo.
