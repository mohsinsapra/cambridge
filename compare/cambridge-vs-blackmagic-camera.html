CamBridge vs Blackmagic Camera

By Mohsin Sapra · October 8, 2026

Both apps turn your iPhone into a serious camera. They solve different problems. Blackmagic Camera is a deep, free manual camera app for phones. CamBridge is a Mac-first recording tool: you control the iPhone from your desk, and the full-quality file lands on your Mac. This guide is for creators searching for the best way to record iPhone video to a Mac.

Quick verdict

  • Pick Blackmagic Camera if you want deep manual control (Log, ProRes, LUTs, higher frame rates), Android support, or Blackmagic Cloud and DaVinci Resolve workflows.
  • Pick CamBridge if you mostly record yourself and want to watch, control and receive footage on your Mac, with no accounts and no cloud.
  • They are not mutually exclusive. Use Blackmagic Camera when the shot needs cinema-style controls, and CamBridge when the Mac is your studio.

What each app is

Blackmagic Camera is a free app from Blackmagic Design for iPhone and iPad, and for Samsung and Google Pixel devices on Android. It exposes manual controls, records to the phone or to an external SSD, and can send files to Blackmagic Cloud for DaVinci Resolve projects. A free Blackmagic ID is needed to use Cloud.

CamBridge is a pair of apps, CamBridge Camera on iPhone and CamBridge Studio on Mac. The iPhone records on-device and the Mac shows a live monitor and sends remote commands. When you stop recording, the full-quality file transfers to a Footage folder you choose on the Mac. It needs no accounts and no cloud.

Solo YouTube recording

If you talk to the camera from a desk, the hard part is not the sensor. It is checking focus, exposure and framing while you are in front of the lens. CamBridge puts a live monitor on your Mac and lets you click to focus, lock AE/AF, change exposure and zoom, and start or stop recording from the keyboard. The file arrives on the Mac when you stop. Blackmagic Camera gives you more image controls on the phone itself, but you adjust them on the phone. For solo desk recording, CamBridge removes more steps.

Vertical shorts

Both handle vertical. CamBridge works in portrait and landscape and keeps an untouched 16:9 master alongside your chosen aspect ratio (16:9, 4:3 or 1:1), which is handy when you repurpose clips. Blackmagic Camera lists vertical recording and frame guides including 9:16 and 4:5.

Podcasts and audio

CamBridge supports DJI Mic over Bluetooth or its receiver, USB mics on the phone, and it can record a USB or podcast mic on the Mac, then give you one finished video with that mic's sound, already in sync. Blackmagic Camera supports external mics and lists AAC, PCM and IEEE Float audio formats, which suits post-production audio workflows.

Multi-cam

CamBridge shows several phones in a grid in Studio and offers a Director mode that uses a second iPhone as a monitor and remote. Blackmagic documents a more formal multi-camera ecosystem: multiview, timecode input from a Bluetooth generator, and automatic clip sync in DaVinci Resolve. If you edit in Resolve with several cameras, Blackmagic has the stronger story.

Pro manual control

This is Blackmagic's territory. Log color, LUTs, ProRes, high frame rates, anamorphic desqueeze and scopes are well beyond what CamBridge offers today. CamBridge is deliberately simpler: SDR by default, optional 10-bit HLG HDR, and the controls a solo creator uses most.

Video calls and streaming

Neither is built as a webcam. CamBridge is not a virtual camera for Zoom or OBS. If that is your need, see our CamBridge vs Camo comparison.

Which should you choose?

  • Cinema-style control, Android, or Cloud and Resolve workflows: Blackmagic Camera.
  • Recording yourself and managing everything from the Mac, with no accounts: CamBridge.
  • Both: install both. They do not conflict.

FAQ

Is Blackmagic Camera really free?

Blackmagic Design lists the Blackmagic Camera app as free on the App Store, Google Play and Galaxy Store, as of October 2026. Some hardware accessories and DaVinci Resolve Studio are paid.

Can I control Blackmagic Camera from my Mac like CamBridge?

Blackmagic describes remote control of compatible Blackmagic cameras from a phone, tablet or Mac on the same network. For a Mac live monitor and remote for the phone app itself, check Blackmagic's documentation.

Does CamBridge record in ProRes or Log?

No. CamBridge records HEVC at 4K with 24, 30 or 60 fps, SDR by default, with optional 10-bit HLG HDR.

Do I need an account for CamBridge?

No. CamBridge connects your iPhone and Mac directly over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. There is no account and no cloud.

Does CamBridge work on Android or Windows?

No. CamBridge needs an iPhone on iOS 17 or later and a Mac on macOS 14 or later.

Can I use both apps?

Yes. Use Blackmagic Camera for shots that need manual or Log workflows and CamBridge when you want desk control and automatic transfer to your Mac.
