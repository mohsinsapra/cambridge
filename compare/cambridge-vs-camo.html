CamBridge vs Camo

By Mohsin Sapra · October 8, 2026

Camo and CamBridge both connect an iPhone to a Mac, but for opposite jobs. Camo makes your phone a webcam for calls and streams. CamBridge makes your phone a camera you record with, controlled from the Mac. If you are choosing between them, start with what you need to do.

Quick verdict

  • Pick Camo for Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord or OBS: it works as a virtual camera in those apps.
  • Pick CamBridge to record high-quality footage (YouTube, shorts, interviews) and get the full-quality file on your Mac.
  • CamBridge is not a webcam or virtual camera. It will not show up as a camera in Zoom or OBS.

What each app is

Camo comes from Reincubate. Camo Camera runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and Camo Studio runs on Mac and Windows. Studio acts as a virtual camera in more than 40 apps, including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord and OBS, and adds effects, overlays and scenes. It also supports recording and streaming from Studio.

CamBridge is a free TestFlight beta with CamBridge Camera on iPhone and CamBridge Studio on Mac. The phone records on-device, the Mac shows a live monitor and controls the camera remotely, and the finished file transfers to your Mac automatically.

Video calls and streaming

Camo wins here, and it is not close. It is built to be a camera that other apps can select. Its free tier covers 720p, and Pro unlocks 1080p and up to 4K. CamBridge does not do this and is not trying to.

Solo YouTube recording

You can record in Camo Studio, but a stream sent over a connection is a different thing from a file recorded on the phone. CamBridge records HEVC at up to 4K on the iPhone itself and transfers that file to your Mac after you stop. Check Camo's documentation to see whether its recordings use the phone's original file, if this matters to you. If your final deliverable is a video file, CamBridge is built around that path.

Vertical shorts

CamBridge supports portrait and landscape and keeps an untouched 16:9 master next to 16:9, 4:3 or 1:1 output. Camo Studio has profiles that set resolution, framing and aspect ratio for a recording.

Podcasts

CamBridge can record a USB or podcast mic on the Mac, sync it automatically and give you one finished video. It also supports DJI Mic over Bluetooth or receiver and USB mics. Camo works with the audio your calling or streaming app uses.

Multi-cam

CamBridge Studio shows multiple phones in a grid, and Director mode uses a second iPhone as monitor and remote. Camo supports connecting a range of cameras into Studio for live scenes. For recording several iPhones to files, CamBridge is the more direct fit. For switching cameras live in a stream, Camo is.

Pro manual control

Neither is a cinema camera app. For Log, ProRes and LUT workflows on the phone, see CamBridge vs Blackmagic Camera.

Which should you choose?

  • You take calls or stream: Camo.
  • You record footage and want the full-quality file on the Mac: CamBridge.
  • You do both: they can live side by side. Use Camo for meetings and CamBridge for recording.

FAQ

Can CamBridge be used as a webcam in Zoom or OBS?

No. CamBridge is for recording footage, not for calls or streaming. It is not a virtual camera. Use Camo or Apple's Continuity Camera for that.

Is Camo free?

Camo has a free tier that Reincubate describes as having no watermark. It covers 720p at 30 fps. Camo Pro unlocks 1080p and up to 4K and more features. See camo.com/pricing for current Pro prices.

Does Camo record to a file?

Camo Studio supports recording and streaming, according to Camo's site. Check Camo's documentation for the exact format and quality of recordings.

Does CamBridge work with Windows or Android?

No. CamBridge needs an iPhone on iOS 17+ and a Mac on macOS 14+. Camo supports Windows and Android.

Does CamBridge work over USB?

Yes. The Mac connects over Wi-Fi or a USB cable, and CamBridge detects which automatically.

Can I use Camo and CamBridge together?

Yes, though not at the same moment on the same phone camera. Use Camo for live calls and CamBridge when you want to record.
