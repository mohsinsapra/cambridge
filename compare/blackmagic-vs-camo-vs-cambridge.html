Blackmagic Camera vs Camo vs CamBridge: which iPhone camera app for Mac creators?

By Mohsin Sapra · October 8, 2026

These three apps all put an iPhone camera to work with a computer, but they do three different jobs. Pick by the job, not by the feature list. We make CamBridge, so we say plainly where the others are the better choice.

Quick verdict

  • Blackmagic Camera: best for deep manual control, Log and ProRes, Android, and Blackmagic Cloud or DaVinci Resolve workflows.
  • Camo: best for using your phone as a webcam in calls and streams.
  • CamBridge: best for recording yourself on iPhone while you monitor and control everything from your Mac, with the file delivered to a folder.

How we compared

We read each vendor's own pages for Blackmagic Camera and Camo, and we describe CamBridge from its current beta. Where we could not confirm a detail, such as Camo Pro prices or Camo's orientation support, we say so instead of guessing. We did not run benchmarks, so there are no speed or quality scores here.

The three jobs

  • Pro phone camera: Blackmagic Camera. You operate the phone, with cinema-style tools.
  • Webcam: Camo. The phone feeds live video into Zoom, OBS and other apps.
  • Desk-controlled recorder: CamBridge. The phone records, the Mac controls and receives.

Solo YouTube recording

CamBridge is the most direct: monitor on the Mac, click to focus, record, and find the file on the Mac. Blackmagic Camera is the choice if you want to grade Log footage in Resolve. Camo is not aimed at this.

Vertical shorts

All three can work with vertical. CamBridge handles portrait and landscape and keeps a 16:9 master. Blackmagic lists vertical and 9:16 frame guides. Camo Studio has aspect-ratio profiles.

Podcasts

CamBridge adds Mac-mic recording merged into the clip, plus DJI Mic and USB mic support. Blackmagic supports external mics and professional audio formats. Camo relies on the app you are streaming into.

Video calls and streaming

Camo. Full stop. CamBridge is not a virtual camera.

Multi-cam

CamBridge offers a multi-phone grid and a Director mode. Blackmagic offers multiview, timecode input and Resolve sync. Camo supports switching sources live in scenes.

Pro manual control

Blackmagic Camera, clearly. It documents Log color on supported phones, LUT support, ProRes on iPhone and iPad, high frame rates and monitoring tools such as zebra, histogram and focus assist. CamBridge keeps to the essentials: exposure, focus, zoom, lens choice, stabilization, frame rate and an optional 10-bit HLG HDR mode.

Which should you choose?

  • I need Log, ProRes or Android: Blackmagic Camera.
  • I need a better webcam: Camo.
  • I record myself and want a Mac-first workflow with no accounts: CamBridge.

FAQ

Which is best for a video call?

Camo. It works as a virtual camera in Zoom, Meet, Teams and other apps. CamBridge does not.

Which is best for recording YouTube videos from my desk?

CamBridge if you want Mac monitoring, remote control and automatic transfer. Blackmagic Camera if you want Log or ProRes and are comfortable operating the phone.

Which apps are free?

Blackmagic Camera is listed as free by Blackmagic Design. Camo has a free tier with a 720p limit plus a paid Pro. CamBridge is a free TestFlight beta.

Which works on Android and Windows?

Blackmagic Camera and Camo support Android, and Camo Studio supports Windows. CamBridge is iPhone and Mac only.

Can I combine them?

Yes. Many creators will want one for calls and another for recording.

Does CamBridge need an account or cloud?

No. It connects directly to your Mac over Wi-Fi or USB.

Try CamBridge free

CamBridge is in free TestFlight beta. It needs iOS 17+ on the iPhone and macOS 14+ on the Mac.
