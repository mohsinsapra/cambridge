Privacy Policy

Your video never leaves your network.

CamBridge Camera (iPhone) and CamBridge Studio (Mac) are built to work entirely on your local network, between your own devices. Your video, audio, and recordings never leave your devices. The only thing the apps send over the internet is anonymous usage statistics, described below. This page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect: anonymous usage statistics only

The apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to count how many people install and use CamBridge. It records events such as the app being opened, a camera connecting, a recording starting or finishing, or a photo being taken. It also records basic technical details: app version, OS version, device type, and an approximate country/region that Google derives from the IP address. These statistics are tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to your identity: no name, email, account, contact, device name, or file name is collected, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. The data is used only to understand how many people use CamBridge and which features matter. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and never sold or shared with data brokers. Google processes it under the Firebase privacy terms.

Video and control data stay on your local network

The video feed and every control signal — focus, zoom, exposure, torch, and the rest — travel directly over Wi‑Fi between your iPhone and your Mac. This connection never leaves your local network and never touches the internet.

Recordings stay on your devices

Anything you capture or record is saved locally on the device that captured it — your iPhone or your Mac. Recordings are never uploaded anywhere by CamBridge.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

There's no sign‑up and no account of any kind. CamBridge contains no advertising. The only third‑party components are Firebase Analytics (both apps) and Sentry (both apps), which we use for crash and error reporting. If an app crashes, hangs, or hits an error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry (hosted in the EU) so we can fix the bug: the stack trace, recent app events, app and OS version, device model (never the name you gave your device), and a few non‑identifying technical details such as the connection type and video codec in use. A small sample of anonymous performance timings is included too. Reports contain no names, file names, recordings, screenshots, or network addresses of your devices, and we do not send personal data with them. Video, audio, recordings, and camera control data are never sent to us or to any third party.

This website (not the apps)

This marketing website uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — roughly how many people visit and where they come from. IP addresses are anonymized, and Google advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled. If you sign up for CamBridge updates, the email address you enter, the channel and platform you pick, and an optional use‑case note are stored so we can email you about CamBridge availability and release news. They are never shared or sold. To be removed, email the address below.
